      Tamper with the evidence (DEMO-ONLY)

      These buttons use a separate administrator connection to edit the log behind the application's back, to show what verification catches. The application's own role cannot do this.

      • Edit: changes one record's contents. The chain check fails at that record.
      • Rewrite: also recomputes every hash, so the chain looks consistent. Only the anchor, kept outside the database, gives it away.
      • Delete: removes a record. The chain check reports a gap at its position.

      Decisions are made by the policy file, or by a person whose name is a free-text label. A model proposes tool requests; it never decides. This page reports control status and evidence of what happened; it is not legal advice.
